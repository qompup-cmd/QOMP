Recent milestones of the group.

August 7, 2026 · SNI–SENACYT

Prof. Dr. Alexis Chacón has been awarded the Category II National Researcher distinction by Panama’s National Research System (SNI–SENACYT), in recognition of his scientific career in the theoretical physics of ultrafast spectroscopy: the study, control and characterization of gases and solid-state matter through light–matter interaction.

May 2026

New preprint on symmetry breaking by quantum light in solid-state high-harmonic generation (arXiv:2605.28236), in international collaboration.

April 2026

Submitted to Physical Review Letters: field-driven helicity in high-harmonic generation, with group alumni Carlos Batista and Jean Paul Menotti as first authors (arXiv:2604.27750).

November 2025

Published in Nature Communications: quantum interference and occupation control in high-harmonic generation from monolayer WS₂, with participation of the group.

2025

Daniela Vargas, Bryan Lorenzo and Carlos Batista defended their B.Sc. theses developed within the group. Congratulations!

October 2025

Talk at the University of Panama congress: “All Nonlinear optical emission from Bulk and Surface States of Bi₂Se₃”.

July 2024

Prof. Dr. Alexis Chacón joins the Department of Physics of the University of Panama and the National Research System (SNI–SENACYT). The QOMP group is born.
