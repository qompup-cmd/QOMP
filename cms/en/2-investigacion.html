Numerical simulation by the group: nonlinear optical response of a quantum material.

Research lines

Theoretical and computational research on light–matter interaction in gas and solid phases, driven by lasers in the terahertz and mid-infrared range.

Attosecond physics and strong fields

Ultrafast electron dynamics in atoms, molecules and solids: ionization, attosecond time delays and the strong-field approximation (1 as = 10⁻¹⁸ s).

High-order harmonic generation in solids

Harmonic emission as an all-optical spectroscopy of electronic structure: semiconductor Bloch equations and the Wannier–Bloch approach.

Quantum and topological materials

2D and 3D topological insulators (Bi₂Se₃), Chern insulators, Dirac, Weyl and multi-Weyl semimetals, Kagome lattices and quantum Hall phases; optical detection of topological phase transitions.

Two-dimensional materials

Graphene, hexagonal boron nitride and transition-metal dichalcogenides (MoS₂, WS₂, WSe₂): layer, symmetry and valley effects in the nonlinear optical response.

Spin and optical phase control

Materials with strong spin–orbit coupling: manipulation of spin currents and optical control of conductor–insulator transitions.

High-performance computing

Numerical solution of the time-dependent Schrödinger equation in 1D–3D, C/C++ with MPI and OpenMP, Matlab, Mathematica and Python.
